================================================================================
   RISHIKA BLOOMS - COMPLETE WEBSITE DEPLOYMENT FOLDER
================================================================================

Ye folder aapki PURI WEBSITE ka final ready-to-run package hai.
Isme sabhi HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Images, aur Routing files shaamil hain.

Aap is folder ko 3 tariko se 24/7 hamesha ke liye online chala sakte hain:

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TARIKA 1: NETLIFY (SABSE AASAN & 100% FREE FOREVER)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Apne browser me ye link kholein:
   https://app.netlify.com/drop

2. Is pure 'PURI_WEBSITE_READY_TO_UPLOAD' folder ko uthakar browser window me
   DRAG and DROP kar dein.

3. 10-20 seconds me Netlify aapko ek free live link de dega:
   (Jaise: https://rishika-blooms.netlify.app)

4. Website 24 ghante, 365 din hamesha ke liye bina ruke chalti rahegi!

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TARIKA 2: HOSTINGER / CPANEL (AGAR AAPKE PAAS DOMAIN & HOSTING HAI)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Apne Hostinger hPanel ya cPanel me login karein.
2. 'File Manager' me jayein aur 'public_html' folder kholein.
3. Bahar bani hui 'PURI_WEBSITE_READY_TO_UPLOAD.zip' file ko upload karein.
4. Upload hone ke baad wahi par 'Extract' (Unzip) kar dein.
5. Saari files seedha public_html ke andar honi chahiye (jaise index.html, assets, images).
6. Aapki website aapke custom domain (e.g. yourname.com) par hamesha ke liye shuru ho jayegi!

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TARIKA 3: VERCEL (FREE FOREVER & SUPER FAST)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. https://vercel.com par jayein aur apna free account banayein.
2. 'Add New Project' par click karke is folder ko upload/connect karein.
3. 30 seconds me website live ho jayegi.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
NOTE:
- Is folder me '.htaccess' aur '_redirects' pehle se shamil hain,
  jisse koi bhi page refresh karne par 404 error nahi aayega!
================================================================================
