Ye folder aapki PURI WEBSITE ka bilkul naya aur updated package hai jisme

Is naye version se:
- Jab aap apne PHONE se WhatsApp number, store details, PIN ya naya bouquet badlenge,
  to wo turant DUSRE PHONES aur COMPUTERS par bina reload kiye 1 second me live badal jayega!

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1: NETLIFY PAR UPDATE / UPLOAD KAREIN (FREE & INSTANT)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Apne browser me ye link kholein:
   https://app.netlify.com/drop
   (Ya apne Netlify dashboard me 'Deploys' tab me jayein)

2. Is folder 'PURI_WEBSITE_READY_TO_UPLOAD' ko uthakar browser me
   DRAG and DROP kar dein.

3. 15-20 seconds me Netlify website ko live update kar dega!

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2: PEHLI BAAR PHONE SE CLOUD SYNC KAREIN (ONE-TIME)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Apne phone me website kholein.
2. URL ke aage #leader ya #owner lagayein:
   (Example: https://rishika-blooms.netlify.app#leader)
3. Apna Owner PIN daalein (Default PIN: risika2026).
4. 'Store Settings & Management' modal me sabse aakhiri tab '☁️ Cloud' par jayein.
5. 'Sync Catalog to Cloud' (क्लाउड सिंक) button par click karein.
6. Bas! Ab saare 100+ bouquets aur categories Google Cloud Database par live ho jayenge.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3: TEST KAREIN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Phone se WhatsApp number ya koi price badal kar 'Save' karein.
- Kisi dusre phone ya laptop par website kholein — wahan turant naya number dikhai dega!
